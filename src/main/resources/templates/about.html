About Linkr

The story, the people, the why.

What is Linkr?

Linkr is a puzzle game where you figure out what connects three images. Given three pictures, your job is to find the hidden link — the word, phrase, or concept that ties them all together. Think of it like 4 Pics 1 Word, but the challenge is lateral thinking and wordplay rather than a single obvious answer. New challenges drop every day, so there's always something fresh to figure out.

How it started

Linkr was born out of frustration. The creator was a fan of the Sidemen — specifically their MoreSidemen channel on YouTube — where the group plays a "Guess the Link" game. After looking around and finding nothing quite like it online, the decision was simple: build it.

The team

Linkr is a solo side project by Thomas Franco, a Cybersecurity student at Holy Angel University in the Philippines. It started as a way to put Java knowledge into practice and ship something real to the world.
