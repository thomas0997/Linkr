How to Play
Linkr

Three images. One hidden connection. Figure it out — the faster, the better.

1

You're shown 3 images

Every round gives you three pictures. They all share a hidden link — a word, phrase, category, or concept that connects them through wordplay or cultural references.

Example question
Sole of a shoe
Brussels sprouts
Deli meat shop
→ Answer: Capital Cities

Sole → Seoul  •  Brussels sprouts → Brussels  •  Deli → Delhi.
Each image sounds like or represents a capital city.

2

Type your guess

You have 10 tries. The placeholder in the input box shows you how many words and how many letters the answer has — use that as a guide. Each wrong guess fills in one of the 10 dots.

3

Use clues if you're stuck

Three clues are available, each getting progressively more obvious. They cost coins — spend wisely. You can also reveal one random letter at a time for 10 coins.

Clue 12 🪙 — vague category
Clue 24 🪙 — narrows it down
Clue 38 🪙 — almost gives it away
Letter10 🪙 — reveals one letter
Capital Cities — clues in order
Clue 1: Think: places

4

Earn coins based on how fast you guess

The fewer tries it takes, the more coins you earn. Coins let you unlock clues on future questions.

5

Climb the leaderboard

The leaderboard ranks players by level reached. Complete more levels to rise — but you only unlock the next level by solving the current one. There are no shortcuts.
